# Persona Dossier Template

Target ~1,400 words. This depth is the whole game — the accuracy Brooke reports (~85-92% correlation with human focus groups) comes from the dossier, not the prompt. Thin personas give confident garbage.

Every panel needs a MIX: struggling and successful versions of the ICP, and 1-2 deliberate wrong-fit personas (people who SHOULD say no — beginners for advanced offers, out-of-market lookalikes). Wrong-fit "no"s prove targeting is working.

```markdown
---
type: synthetic-persona
project: {biz-slug}
persona_id: {biz}-{segment}{n}
tags: [biz/{biz}, theme/marketing, status/active, synthetic-persona]
date: YYYY-MM-DD
version: 1.0
---

# Persona: "{Archetype Label}" — {Full Name}

## Demographics
Age, location, marital/family status, occupation, income (personal + household),
education, ethnicity, relevant identity/background details.

## Life / Family / Business Context
The concrete situation they operate in — the details that shape what they can
buy, when they can act, and who else weighs in on decisions.

## Professional Background
How they got here; how they operate; what they're known for; their relationship
to spending money on solutions (burned before? proven ROI buyer?).

## Goals
- **Primary goal:** the specific outcome they'd pay for
- **Secondary goal:** the supporting/adjacent want
- **Implicit goal:** the emotional/identity outcome they won't say out loud

## Pain Points
5-7 specific, situational pains — bolded label + 2-3 sentences each. Not
categories ("time management") but scenes ("her son goes from confident to
tight in about 2 innings and she sees it in his body language before it happens").

## Emotional Frustrations
**What keeps them up at night** — 4-6 first-person quotes in their actual voice.

## Empathy Map
- **Thinking:** …
- **Feeling:** …
- **Seeing:** … (their feeds, their peers, their environment)
- **Saying:** … (what they say publicly vs. privately)
- **Doing:** … (actual behaviors, incl. past purchase attempts)
- **Pains:** … / **Gains:** …

## Platforms & Information Diet
Where they scroll, who they trust, what content formats they consume,
what ads they've been shown lately.

## Decision-Making Process
How they go from aware → considering → buying. Who they consult. What proof
they need. Typical objections in order. Price sensitivity and framing
(save-money vs. make-money vs. status vs. safety).

## Vocabulary
10-15 words/phrases THEY use for their problem (and words that feel foreign/
salesy to them). The copywriter mirrors this section.

## Buying Posture (for offer-mode yes/no)
What tier of offer fits them today ($, $$, $$$), and what would make this
persona a WRONG fit for other tiers.
```
